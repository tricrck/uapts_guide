# Railway — module guide

_Railway_

SGR and MGR rail operations: train positions and delays, freight volumes and port-rail reconciliation, timetables, track and signalling assets, network inventory and rail safety.

## Pages in this module

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Railway Overview](railway-overview.md) | SGR & MGR ridership, freight manifests, KPA port-rail reconciliation, KenTrade cargo, and NCTTCA corridor KPIs |
| [Train Operations](train-operations.md) | Real-time SGR &amp; MGR train positions, delays, rolling stock status, and NCTTCA corridor transit performance - refreshes every 30 seconds |
| [Freight Volumes](freight-volumes.md) | Freight manifests, corridor analysis, KenTrade single-window clearances, NCTTCA transit tracking, and KPA port-rail reconciliation |
| [Schedules](schedules.md) | SGR &amp; MGR timetables, OTP, NCTTCA corridor punctuality benchmarks, and recent operation adherence |
| [Rail Infrastructure](rail-infrastructure.md) | Track section condition, level crossings, signalling, culverts, and the capital works pipeline |
| [Rail Network Inventory](rail-network-inventory.md) | Line, station and rolling-stock registry with an SGR/MGR comparison panel and cross-network data quality checks |
| [Rail Safety](rail-safety.md) | Incident tracking, level crossing safety, regulatory compliance, and predictive risk indicators |
