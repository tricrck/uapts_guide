# Road Infrastructure — module guide

_Road Infrastructure_

Road network inventory and condition, construction and maintenance programmes, bridges and roadside assets, and funding allocations by agency and fiscal year.

## Pages in this module

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Overview](infrastructure-overview.md) | network condition, assets, projects and budget at a glance |
| [Road Network Inventory](road-network-inventory.md) | Per-agency network inventory, pavement quality (IRI/PCI), and AI deterioration forecasts |
| [Road Infrastructure Status](road-infrastructure-status.md) | KeNHA · KURA · KeRRA · LAPSSET · KRB (funding) - Construction portfolio, maintenance work orders, asset status, and exceptions per agency |
| [Bridges & Assets](bridges-and-assets.md) | Bridge inventory, condition scores, inspection schedules, streetlights, signals, WIM stations and closures |

| [Maintenance Orders](maintenance-orders.md) | Road maintenance work orders, contractor progress, KRB budget allocation, and utilization tracking |

## Who can use it

Module **M06 · Road Infrastructure**. Agencies with full access in the baseline policy: KENHA, KURA, KERRA, SDR, KRB, SDT.

## Support

- **Your agency administrator** is the first contact for access, role and data-quality questions.
- **Data feeds, templates and API questions:** `api-support@uapts.go.ke` (include the `X-Request-ID` response header from a failing call).
- **Security concerns:** `security@uapts.go.ke`. Do not report suspected vulnerabilities publicly.
