Welcome to UAPTS

Getting started › Welcome

UAPTS — the Unified Analytics and Predictive Transport System — brings the data of Kenya's transport agencies into one place: roads, public transport, rail, maritime and aviation. Each agency keeps ownership of its data; UAPTS collects it, checks it, and shows it as live dashboards, maps, forecasts and reports for planners, regulators, operators and analysts.

🎬 Video tutorial: coming soon.

Who this guide is for

  • Operators and officers who monitor traffic, fleets, incidents, ports, rail and flights day to day.
  • Analysts who explore data, run the Query Builder, use forecasts and generate reports.
  • Agency administrators who manage their agency's users, upload data and register live feeds.
  • Super administrators who oversee every agency, the data feeds and the audit trail.

How the guide is organised

The left-hand menu of this guide follows the sidebar of the UAPTS web application, group by group. Each page of the guide matches one screen of the application and always follows the same pattern:

  • A short summary and a list of highlights.
  • Enabling and access — how to open the screen and who may see it.
  • A Page tour with a screenshot of every section, and for every tile, chart and table: which API endpoint supplies it and which upload template or live feed loads the underlying records.
  • Working with this page — numbered steps.
  • Status and value meanings, Permissions, Related topics, an On this page index and Support contacts.

Where the data comes from

Nothing on a UAPTS screen is typed in by the page itself. Records reach the platform in one of three ways, and the guide names the route for each data point:

Route What it looks like Where to find it
Upload template An agency fills a locked Excel/CSV template and uploads it in the Integration Hub. Rows are validated, previewed and committed. Data sources catalogue — every template can be downloaded as .xlsx or .csv.
Live feed A system such as KeNHA's counting stations or NTSA iTIMS pushes records to the ingest API with an API key. Integration Hub → Upload & Connect
Computed by UAPTS Forecasts, black spots, alerts and scores are calculated from other data. Marked “computed by UAPTS” in the Data sources tables.

Where a table has no template or live feed yet, the guide says so plainly (“no template yet”). The full list is in the Data sources catalogue.

The modules

Module What it is for
Command Centre The Command Centre is the landing page after sign-in. Ministry and platform users see the National view; agency users see their own Agency view; and an administrator can assign a custom dashboard to an agency, department, role or person.
Road Traffic Management Live and historical traffic on the national road network: counting-station health, congestion events, speed compliance, vehicle mix, weather impact, origin–destination flows and short-term forecasts.
Fleet & Vehicle Tracking Tracks public-service and government vehicles: utilisation, speed-governor compliance, driver behaviour, live GPS positions, geofence breaches and historical trip replay.
Public Transport Operational and regulatory view of public transport: BRT, PSV licensing and compliance, operators, vehicle registration and inspection, and driver licensing.
Safety & Incidents Road-safety intelligence: national KPIs, active incidents and emergency dispatch, accident black spots and predictive hotspots, and traffic-violation trends.
Road Infrastructure Road network inventory and condition, construction and maintenance programmes, bridges and roadside assets, and funding allocations by agency and fiscal year.
Aviation Airport and airline operations: flight movements, on-time performance, passenger and cargo volumes, aircraft and operator licensing, and aviation infrastructure.
Maritime Ports and shipping: vessel traffic and registry, berth and port operations, cargo and trade flows, waterways, maritime safety, green-transport indicators and port performance ranking.
Railway SGR and MGR rail operations: train positions and delays, freight volumes and port-rail reconciliation, timetables, track and signalling assets, network inventory and rail safety.
Corridor & Trade Northern Corridor and LAPSSET trade data: transit declarations, one-stop border-post performance, corridor KPIs and corridor development.
Analytics & Reporting Predictive models, ad-hoc querying and report generation across all modules.
Integration Hub Where agency data enters UAPTS: upload spreadsheets using locked templates, register live feeds, review every batch and monitor whether data is actually landing.
GIS & Spatial Analysis A national map explorer for road, rail, public-transport and port layers, plus the catalogue, coverage tracker and upload requests for every agency's spatial layers.
Training Institutes Accredited transport and safety training: course catalogue, cohorts, enrolments and certificates.
Notifications Real-time alerts from every module, and the rules that decide who is alerted and when.
Access Control Manage who can sign in and what they can reach: agencies, users, roles, module access, custom dashboards and the audit trail.

Roles at a glance

Role Typical use
Public Read-only access to published dashboards.
Operator Day-to-day operations: acknowledge alerts, update incidents, submit uploads.
Analyst Everything an operator can read, plus export, Query Builder and reports.
Admin Manages the agency's users, approves uploads, registers feeds and configures alert rules.
Super admin Platform-wide: every agency, every setting.

See Roles & Permissions for the complete list.
