# Corridor & Trade — module guide

_Corridor & Trade_

Northern Corridor and LAPSSET trade data: transit declarations, one-stop border-post performance, corridor KPIs and corridor development.

## Pages in this module

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Overview](corridor-overview.md) | transit, border crossings and corridor development across the Northern and LAPSSET corridors |
| [Northern Corridor](northern-corridor.md) | Mombasa Port to the Great Lakes region - Transport Observatory indicators against benchmark, route condition and the port-to-border transit chain |
| [Border Crossings](border-crossings.md) | One-stop border posts - queues, waiting and clearance times, truck volumes and the main cause of delay |
| [Transit Declarations](transit-declarations.md) | Cargo moving through Kenya under a transit declaration - customs status, destination, time in transit and security alerts |
| [LAPSSET Corridor](lapsset-corridor.md) | Lamu Port – South Sudan – Ethiopia Transport corridor - port operations, highway and railway construction, and investment against plan |

## Who can use it

Module **M15 · Corridor and Trade**. Agencies with full access in the baseline policy: SDR, SDT, NCTTCA, LAPSSET; read-only: KENTRADE.
